Weekend baker in Toronto. This is my corner of the web.

About me

I started baking bread in 2020, like everyone else, and never stopped. These days I bake most Saturdays and give most of it away.

What I bake

  • Sourdough and rye bread
  • Birthday cakes, for friends who ask nicely
  • Cinnamon buns, only in winter

My Saturday

  1. Feed the starter on Friday night.
  2. Mix the dough first thing in the morning.
  3. Bake in the afternoon, then share.

Where I learned

  • Sourdough, on Wikipedia
  • King Arthur Baking's recipes
