Sourdough bread

You'll need

  • 500 g bread flour
  • 350 g water
  • 100 g active starter
  • 10 g salt

Method

  1. Mix everything and leave it covered for an hour.
  2. Fold the dough every half hour, four times.
  3. Shape it, then leave it in the fridge overnight.
  4. Bake at 240 °C in a covered pot for 45 minutes, uncovering it for the last 15.

Wet your hands before folding, and the dough won't stick to them.
